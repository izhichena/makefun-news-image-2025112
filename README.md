# The World Today — 25 November 2026

A single newspaper-style image summarising the world news of 25 November 2026. Run `python3 main.py` to write the image to `/output/entry.png`.

## News sources (25 November 2026)

1. **Hormuz: 2 tankers hit mines** — Iran's IRGC said two oil tankers struck naval mines, exploded and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz. Source: Just Security, Early Edition: November 25, 2026 (https://www.justsecurity.org/165910/early-edition-november-25-2026/)
2. **Ukraine: Strike on Odesa** — Ukraine's Odesa region governor said a Russian strike killed two people and injured three, including a child; Kyiv was also attacked. Source: Just Security, Early Edition: November 25, 2026 (https://www.justsecurity.org/165910/early-edition-november-25-2026/)
3. **Huawei convicted in US** — A US federal jury found Huawei guilty of conspiracy to steal trade secrets and bank fraud, but not guilty of racketeering. Source: Just Security / Principia News, 25 November 2026 (https://principia.news/e/2026-11-25)
4. **Asia markets plunge** — Asian stocks tumbled: South Korea's KOSPI plunged 6.16%, Japan's Nikkei fell 2.78%, Taiwan's Taiex fell ~5%; on Wall Street the Dow closed above 50,000 for the first time. Source: AP / Reuters via "What Happened in the World in the Last 24 Hours | November 25, 2026" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wHDpJ0gTDgE)
5. **Australia–Solomons treaty** — Australia and the Solomon Islands signed a treaty precluding a foreign military or security base in the Solomon Islands. Source: Just Security, Early Edition: November 25, 2026 (https://www.justsecurity.org/165910/early-edition-november-25-2026/)
6. **Shenzhou-24 docks in space** — China's Shenzhou-24 docked with the Tiangong space station about 3.5 hours after launch, carrying Pakistan's first astronaut. Source: China Manned Space Agency via Open Chronicle Global News Brief, November 25, 2026 (https://openchronicle.org/2026/11/25/global-news-brief-key-developments-around-the-world-november-25-2026/)
